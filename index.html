Hey, we're Sunset Scoops!

About the Crew

Sunset Scoops started in summer 2025 when a group of friends in the Outer Sunset decided to turn their love of ice cream into a weekend adventure. We're all between 10 and 14, and our parents help with the grown-up stuff (health permits, buying supplies, and making sure we don't eat all the inventory).

A portion of every scoop goes to Ocean Beach cleanup efforts. Scoops for a cause!
