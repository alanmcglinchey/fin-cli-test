## Who We Are

Sunset Scoops is a crew of neighborhood kids from the Outer Sunset in San Francisco. We started in summer 2025 when we decided to turn our love of ice cream into a weekend adventure.

We are all between 10 and 14 years old. Our parents help with the grown-up stuff like health permits, buying supplies, and making sure we do not eat all the inventory.

## Our Mission

We want to bring smiles to the Outer Sunset, one scoop at a time! We also donate a portion of every scoop to Ocean Beach cleanup efforts. Scoops for a cause!

## The Bike

Our ice cream bicycle is a custom cargo bike with a freezer box, a big pink umbrella, and a bell that plays a jingle. You will hear us before you see us!

## Contact Us

The best way to reach us is through the chat on our website. You can also find us on the Great Highway every weekend. Come say hi!
