## Party Packages

We will pedal our ice cream bicycle right to your party anywhere in the Outer Sunset and Parkside neighborhoods!

**Small Party (up to 25 guests):** $150
- Includes 2 scoops per person
- Choice of 3 flavors
- All toppings included
- We bring cups, spoons, napkins, and good vibes

**Big Party (up to 50 guests):** $275
- Includes 2 scoops per person
- Choice of 4 flavors
- All toppings included
- We bring everything you need

**Extra scoops at the party:** $2.50 each

## How to Book

Chat with us through the messenger on our website! Just click the chat icon and let us know:
- The date and time of your party
- The address (must be in Outer Sunset or Parkside)
- How many guests
- Any special requests or allergy info

## How Far in Advance?

Please book at least 1 week in advance so we can make sure we have everything ready. For big parties (over 25 guests), 2 weeks notice is best.

## What Area Do You Cover?

We cover the Outer Sunset and Parkside neighborhoods in San Francisco. That is roughly from Lincoln Way down to Sloat Blvd, and from the Great Highway to about 30th Avenue. If you are not sure if we can reach you, just ask!

## Cancellation

If you need to cancel, just let us know at least 3 days before the party for a full refund. We get it — plans change!
