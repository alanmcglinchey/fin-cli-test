## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are you open during the week?**
No, just weekends! We have school during the week. Saturday and Sunday only.

**What if it is raining?**
We are usually still out there! This is San Francisco — a little fog and drizzle never stopped us. If conditions are really bad we will post on our website.

**Do you have dairy-free options?**
Not yet, but we are working on it! Stay tuned.

**Can I order ahead?**
For parties, yes! Chat with us on the website to book. For weekend walk-ups on the Great Highway, it is first come first served.

**Where exactly on the Great Highway are you?**
Saturday we are near Rivera St, Sunday near Noriega St. Look for the pink umbrella!

**Do you have cones?**
We only serve in cups right now. It is easier to carry on the bike and better for the beach — less mess!

**How do I pay?**
Cash or Venmo! We have a QR code on the bike.

**Can you come to my school or event?**
Maybe! Chat with us and tell us about it. If it is in the Outer Sunset or Parkside area we can probably make it work.

**Is this a real business?**
Yes! We have a health permit and everything. Our parents help with the official stuff but we do all the scooping.

**How can I join the crew?**
We are always looking for more scoopers! If you are a kid in the Outer Sunset and want to help out on weekends, chat with us.
