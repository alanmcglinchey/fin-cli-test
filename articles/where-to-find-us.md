## Weekend Locations

We pedal our ice cream bike along the Great Highway path in the Outer Sunset every weekend!

**Saturday:** 11am to 4pm near Rivera St on the Great Highway path

**Sunday:** 11am to 3pm near Noriega St on the Great Highway path

## How to Spot Us

Look for the bicycle with the big pink umbrella and the Sunset Scoops sign. We are usually on the path between Rivera and Noriega streets, close to the beach.

## Weather

We are out rain or shine (or fog — this is the Sunset after all!). If conditions are really bad we will post an update on our website. If it is actually sunny we might stay out later!

## Can not make it to the Great Highway?

You can book us for your party instead! Just chat with us on our website to arrange it.
